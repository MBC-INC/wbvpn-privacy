# WBVPN Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 8, 2026* · [Русская версия](ru)

WBVPN is a free VPN app for iPhone made by MBCAHF, Inc. ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. The short version: we don't collect any.

## What we collect

Nothing. WBVPN has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no tracking. We don't log your traffic, the sites you visit, your DNS requests, your IP address or when you connect.

## What leaves your phone

- **Your traffic, while you're connected.** It travels encrypted to the VPN server you picked (or the fastest one, with Auto), and from there to the sites you visit. Those sites see the server's IP address instead of yours.
- **Russian sites, if you choose so.** If you turn on "Open them without the VPN" in Settings, Russian sites are reached directly, the same way as with the VPN off. Everything else still goes through the VPN.
- **The server list.** The app downloads the list of free servers. As with any download, the server that hosts the list sees your IP address.
- **Speed checks.** To find the fastest server, the app sends a tiny request to www.gstatic.com through each server. Google sees the server's address, not yours.
- **Update checks.** The app asks Apple's App Store whether a newer version is out.

## What stays on your phone

Your chosen location, your answer about Russian sites, your app icon, the saved server list and the latest speed results. They never leave your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

## Sharing

We never sell, rent, use or share your data with anyone, for any purpose.

## Advertising

This version of WBVPN shows no ads and contains no ad tracking. If a future version adds ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

## Children

WBVPN isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
